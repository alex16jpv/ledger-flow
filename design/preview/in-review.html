The consequence of taking Adjustment out of Add, drawn so it can be seen rather than described. The four types were presented as equals and are not: three of them are things that happened to your money — two of them daily, one weekly — and the fourth is a repair tool for when a balance has drifted. It moves to Adjust balance inside the account, which already exists, already preloads the recorded balance and already explains the difference it is about to write; the segment here drops to three.
