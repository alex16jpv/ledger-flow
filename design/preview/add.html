A phone sheet with a form fills the screen2026-09-23

T-150. His decision of 2026-09-23 that no phone sheet keeps rising from the bottom, and his correction the same day: «no es ponerlo arriba literalmente. el modal debe cambiar su estructura para que no sea abajo no simplemente subirlo. debe de existir algun estandar para modales en movil». This is the standard both platforms share — Material 3’s full-screen dialog, iOS’s sheet with a navigation bar — and the first of the two forms every phone sheet now takes. The rule: a form (more than one field) or a list that scrolls is a full-screen dialog; a question, a short choice, or one field with its button is a centred dialog. Full screen means a 56px bar on top — close on the left, the title, the sheet’s one primary action on the right — and the body scrolling under it, so the keyboard can cover part of the body but never the action. In quick add, Save moves up to the bar and More details goes to the end of the body. The bar that opened the full form goes: there is no sheet edge left to pull (his gesture of 2026-09-15), and More details stays as the way in. Drawn with the numeric keyboard up. From 600px up nothing changes: the same sheet is the centred 520px modal with its footer — switch the device to see it.

Account picker · on an income2026-09-18

The form's half of T-93, whose rule lives in the server. An income cannot land on a card or a loan — money arriving there is a payment, and counted as income it would inflate Income this month, Estimated savings and every income budget with money nobody earned. So under Income the picker leaves those two out, and the note says why rather than leaving someone hunting for their Visa. An overdraft stays, and it is the one debt type that does: his decision of 2026-09-18, because a positive balance is an overdraft's ordinary state and a salary landing there is income. Somewhere else goes with them: money from outside landing on an account that holds money is income, so that row has nothing to offer here. Expense and Transfer are untouched — spending with a card, paying one and a cash advance out of one are all real.

When only the movement lands2026-09-21

The two writes are not atomic — POST /sync applies one operation at a time and online they are two requests — so the movement is written first: it is the money, and it is true whatever the group does. When the group refuses, the form is replaced by what actually happened, because there is nothing left to type; leaving the fields would offer to change an amount already recorded. Add it to the group again retries only the half that is missing. With no network this state does not appear: both writes queue, the expense waits for its movement, and a refusal surfaces in the attention tray.
