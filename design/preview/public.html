Why Ledger Flow

All your money, in one app

Ledger Flow is a free expense tracker and budget app for everyday personal finance. You log what you spend, it keeps every account balance right, it splits the bills you share with other people, and it shows you where your money goes — by category, by month, and against the budgets you set.

Three-second capture

Tap, type the amount, done. It suggests what you wrote before, and the category can wait in your review inbox: the expense already counts.

Budgets that talk back

Weekly, monthly, yearly or your own dates, for everything or per category, with pace and days left in plain language — not just a bar.

Shared expenses

Trips, rent, dinners out: split each bill your way and always know who owes you and how much, across every group.

Stats and trends

Where the money went by category, day, tag or account, and up to a year of income against spending with your savings rate.

Every account you have

Cash, bank accounts, credit and debit cards, savings, investments and loans. Every movement keeps the balance right.

Works without signal

Log on the bus or abroad, and install it on your phone or computer. Everything syncs when you are back online, with nothing duplicated.

Shared expenses

Split bills with friends, family and roommates

A trip, the rent, a night out: put the bill in a shared group, say how it splits, and Ledger Flow keeps the tally for everyone — what each person owes, what they have paid and what is left.

  • Split equally, by percent, by exact amounts, or a fixed part and the rest.
  • See what each person owes you across every group, and what you owe them.
  • Get paid in parts or in cash: money paid back is not counted as income.
  • Add anyone by name: nobody needs an account. If they use Ledger Flow too, invite them with their email and they can see the group.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Ledger Flow free?

Yes. Creating an account costs nothing and asks for no card.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You record your own movements, so Ledger Flow never asks for your bank details or passwords.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. What you record offline is saved on your device and syncs when the connection comes back. Installing the app keeps that copy safe from the browser’s clean-ups.

Do the people I split with need an account?

No. Add anyone to a shared group by name. If they also use Ledger Flow, invite them with the email of their account: the group appears in their Shared, and once you mark their part paid they can add it to their own ledger. Nothing is emailed, so let them know.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

Yes. It runs in the browser of any phone, tablet or computer, installs like an app on Android, iPhone and desktop, and keeps your data in sync across all of them.

Who can see my data?

Only you, and the people you invite to see a shared group. We don’t sell data or use it for advertising, and you can delete your account from Settings.

Privacy policy

Ledger Flow is a free personal finance app. This page explains what we keep about you, why, who helps us run the service, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. It is also our data processing policy under Colombia’s Ley 1581 de 2012.

Who is responsible

Ledger Flow is run by alexpiral, which decides how your data is used and answers for it.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, your email, your password (kept in a form nobody can read back) and your settings.
  • What you record: accounts, categories, transactions and budgets.
  • Shared expenses: the people you add, the groups you create with their expenses and payments, and the invitations you send or receive.
  • The technical data the service needs: the devices you sign in on, your IP address to stop abuse, and a record of the emails we send you.

Who sees what

  • Your records are yours alone, except what you record in a shared group.
  • A group’s owner and members see the name on your profile and the expenses and payments recorded in it; someone you invite sees your name and your email.
  • Only you see the details of the people you add. We never write to them.

Why we use it

Only to run the service you sign up for. We do not sell data, we do not use it for advertising, and nothing about you is decided automatically.

Who helps us run it

  • Amazon Web Services runs our server and sends our emails (Amazon SES).
  • MongoDB Atlas hosts the database.
  • Vercel hosts the website and counts page visits without cookies.
  • Sentry receives error reports, without your name, email or IP address.
  • Cloudflare runs Turnstile, the check that you are a person, as its Turnstile privacy addendum explains.

They keep the data in the United States.

Cookies and your device

Only the cookies the app needs to work, and nothing that tracks you or shows ads. Your device keeps a copy of your data to work offline.

How long we keep it

  • Your data, for as long as you have an account; what you delete is erased for good with it.
  • A deleted account is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, then erased for good.
  • Technical data, 30 days at most.

Your rights

Know, access, update, correct and delete your data, and withdraw your authorization. We answer questions within 10 business days and requests within 15. You can complain to the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio.

Security

Connections are encrypted and we use reasonable measures to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If a change affects who is responsible for your data or what it is used for, we tell you before it applies.

Data processing (Ley 1581 de 2012)

The controller is alexpiral, reached at ledgerflow@alexpiral.com. It applies from October 5, 2026.
