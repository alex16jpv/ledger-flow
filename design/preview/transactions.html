The file, and the template for an import2026-09-24

One format for every language, so the file you download is the file a future import reads: fixed column names, types as codes, dates as 2026-09-22, amounts with a dot. The first eleven are what a person writes, and all a template needs; the next five are written by the app and let an import rebuild a download exactly; the last four are for reading and an import ignores them. Oldest first. amount is negative when money left an account and positive when it arrived — a transfer carries no sign — and a shared expense keeps its full amount there with your_share beside it.

A shared expense · what the guests have paid2026-09-21

A block of guests lives in this expense alone: it is not a person, it never reaches the People face, and it has no page of its own. So its payments are listed here and nowhere else, and each one undoes itself like any other (shared.html#undo-a-payment). Without this list a payment to a block could not be taken back — and the expense could not be deleted either, because the server refuses to lose a block that has paid.

Deleting one people have paid for2026-09-20

The confirmation names what it drags, and the surprising half is what it does not do: no payment is deleted. A payment belongs to the person, not to one expense, so the $1,100,000 that has arrived stays and re-imputes over the four expenses left. What does change is what everybody owes — Ana would be $300,000 ahead of a smaller share — and the sheet says so, and offers writing it off instead.
