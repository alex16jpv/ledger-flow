Notifications

What you would otherwise not see: an invitation to a shared group, an answer to yours, changes in a group you are in. Nothing interrupts — arriving is a count on the bell and a dot on More, and the inbox is where you read it. Budget alerts will be new rows of the same inbox, not a second one.

How it arrives2026-09-22

The only sign: a count on the bell in Home's header, a brand dot on More below 900px, and the count beside Notifications in the sidebar. The amber dot on Transactions stays what it was — your own entries waiting for review — so the two never share a colour.

The inbox2026-09-22
