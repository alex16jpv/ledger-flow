Emails

Every email the app sends, in the language of the account and in the client’s own fonts. One layout for all of them: the brand as text, a title, one or two sentences, the code or the facts, one button, and a grey box for “if it wasn’t you”. No images, nothing a person typed, no tracking. The backend’s templates copy these plates; dark mode is what clients that honour it show.
