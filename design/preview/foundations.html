Foundations

Every colour in the interface comes from 18 OKLCH seeds: one neutral, one brand and the 16 tokens the backend stores. Light and dark are derived from the same seeds with the same rules, so a palette never decides a colour per mode. Switch the palette in the top bar and everything repaints without a component changing.
