Loan detail · and paying it2026-09-17

The other half of his sentence — un botón para pagar la tarjeta o el préstamo — drawn because a loan is not a card. It has no limit, so the bar is what is still owed of what was borrowed and the line under it says how much is paid; its opening balance is the loan itself, so the hero’s last line carries it. Pay this loan opens the same sheet as the card. Two things are true here and not on the card. Paying a loan in full is the rare case, not the common one — the ordinary payment is the monthly instalment — which is why the sheet opening with the whole debt read wrong on this screen; T-99 fixed that for both (`#pay-opens-empty`), and a This month’s payment preset would still need a field the account does not have: the two it gains are the credit limit and the amount borrowed, and neither is an instalment. And the instalment is the place T-86’s capital-and-interest split lands: under that split one payment is two movements, and this sheet writes one.

A debt account that owes nothing2026-09-17

This is where your cards are right now, and it is not T-90. A CARD or an OVERDRAFT whose balance is zero or above owes nothing, and past zero it keeps leading with what it has available (T-101): the money on it is its owner’s, so it is credit to spend on top of the limit. Two accounts are drawn: a card carrying your own $4,000,000 — a limit typed in as a balance, exactly what you described — and an overdraft at $320,000, which is the ordinary state of an overdraft and not a mistake at all. The overdraft has its field, so it reads $2,320,000 available with $0 owed of $2,000,000 · $320,000 of your own money on it under an empty bar: the sentence it already had at exactly zero, with one clause added. The rule has to exist either way: a card can be overpaid, an overdraft normally sits positive, and until T-90 runs every card in the product looks like the first one. Without it the screen would say “$4,000,000 owed” about money you have, and until T-101 it said nothing at all about what was still available. The card is drawn with no limit yet, because that is the whole state on day one: no account carries the field until someone fills it, so it reads $0 owed with the money named, no bar at all — there is no scale to fill — and the prompt still asks for the limit. It is also the honest answer to “why did my total drop”: once this ships, that card stops counting $4,000,000 towards what you have.

Adjust balance · on a card2026-09-17

The same sheet, asking what he decided on 2026-09-17 when he asked whether adjusting a card meant the available credit or the debt: the debt. So Actual balance and its Positive / Negative pair give way to the question the account form already asks — How much do you owe on it right now? — and the line under it reads the recorded state in the product’s own words. The pair does not disappear, it changes what it means: Owed or Your own money, because an overdraft sitting positive is its ordinary state, a card can be overpaid, and until T-90 runs every card is in exactly that state; without it those balances could not be said on the one screen whose job is to say them. The question follows the choice, so the label cannot contradict the answer, and the difference is read as the grammar the rest of the product uses: less owed, not “an adjustment of −$12,300”. Nothing changes in what reaches the server.

Pay this card · as the sheet opens2026-09-18

T-99, his words of 2026-09-17: el valor por defecto no puede ser el pagar el valor total. el valor total debe ser la segunda opcion y el usuario debe decider cuanto es lo que paga. The field used to open holding the whole debt, so the sheet had already decided what you were paying and the only way out was a chip that emptied it again. Now it opens empty, with the keyboard up — what the Quick add does, and what he approved there — and the total is one chip that fills it, carrying its own figure so nothing is hidden by the change. The chip reads as selected while the typed amount is exactly that, so it also answers have I typed all of it?, and it is a switch: pressing it again empties the field, which is the way back Another amount used to be — one tap instead of deleting a seven-figure number by hand. Pay stays disabled and no sentence is read back until there is an amount: there is no movement yet to describe. The second chip goes: Another amount existed only to clear a field that arrived full, and beside an empty focused field it is a control with nothing to do. This is also what `#loan-detail-and-pay` flagged — on a loan the ordinary payment is the instalment, not the whole debt — and the sheet no longer assumes either; what a This month’s payment preset would need is still a figure the account does not carry, which is T-94.

Creating a card · the field that asks for the debt2026-09-17

The form that starts the habit. Today Current balance is a plain amount box, so “my card has a limit of 100” becomes a balance of 100 — which is how the cards got into the state T-90 has to repair. On a CARD, an OVERDRAFT or a LOAN the field asks the question instead: How much do you owe on it right now?, entered as a plain positive figure and stored as the debt, with the credit limit beside it and a preview card that reads back what the account will look like. Every other type keeps the field it has. The alternative was drawn and dropped: keeping “Current balance” with an increase/decrease control beside it, which puts a sign decision in front of someone on the one screen where the whole confusion starts.
