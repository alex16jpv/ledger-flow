Ledger Flow · Design

Every screen of the app, drawn from the same tokens as the code: 155 plates across 19 pages. Use the top bar to switch palette, mode and device — everything repaints without a component changing. The sidebar is the map, and its search box finds a plate by name: type “sync” or “budget”, or press / from anywhere.

Where to start

  • What changed — every plate by the day it arrived, newest first.
  • Foundations — the colour system, the type scale and the icons everything else is built from.
